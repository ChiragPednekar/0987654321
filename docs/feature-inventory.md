# CaseCode — what the platform actually offers

A checklist for competitive comparison. Every row is a **checkable claim**, not
a marketing line, so you can open a competitor and answer yes or no.

All figures verified against the production database on 2026-09-09.

**How to use it:** add a column per competitor. Mark ✓ if they have it, ✗ if
not, and `?` if you cannot tell from public pages. The ✗ column is your
differentiator list; the `?` column is what you need a demo or trial to answer.

## Hypotheses worth testing first

Not claims — the four rows I would check before any others, because if a
competitor matches them the differentiation argument gets much harder:

1. **2.4 — server-recomputed, rubric-clamped scoring.** Most AI graders let the
   model produce the number. Ours cannot exceed the rubric.
2. **6.8 / 6.9 — student practice and instructor marking in one product, with
   the AI advisory and the teacher authoritative.** This is the most plausible
   moat: a student tool and a gradebook are usually different companies.
3. **3.3 — peer interviews where the platform gives the interviewer the model
   answer.** Pairing students is common; arming one side is not.
4. **7.6 — per-institution AI cost reporting.** Nobody selling campus seats
   usually knows their own unit economics per customer.

## Where we are weak — check these honestly too

Worth knowing before someone else tells you:

- **Cases are AI-generated from archetypes**, not written by ex-consultants.
  A competitor with 200 human-authored cases from real MBB interviewers has a
  quality story we do not.
- **No human coaching.** Several competitors sell 1:1 sessions with former
  interviewers — a different and much higher-priced product.
- **No verified outcomes.** No placement statistics, no "X% got offers".
- **Small community.** Peer features need liquidity; a competitor with an
  established forum has network effects we do not.
- **The ChatGPT question.** A student can paste a case into a general model and
  ask for rubric feedback. What we add is the rubric, the case library, the
  progress record and the instructor side — but the answer needs to be honest.
- **Payments are not live.** Pro cannot currently be purchased.
