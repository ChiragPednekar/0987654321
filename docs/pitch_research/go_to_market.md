# Go-to-market strategy — CaseCode

*Phase 8. No partnerships, customers or pilots exist today. Everything
below is a plan.*

---

## 1. Who to approach first, and why

**First target:** Heads of Placement / Corporate Relations at 20–30 private
PGDM/MBA institutes in one metro cluster (ICP in `market_analysis.md` §5).

**Why them:**
- They feel the problem directly (unprepared students, recruiter feedback) and own the outcome.
- They are reachable without procurement for a free pilot.
- A placement head who sees usage data becomes the internal champion for the Director's budget decision.

**Second:** one or two faculty members per institute who teach case-heavy
courses (strategy, marketing, finance), who can use the assignment and
marking loop as coursework. Faculty adoption makes usage happen every week,
not only in placement season.

**Not first:** IIMs and the top-10 schools. They have alumni-run case
preparation, global vendors and long approval chains. They are an
aspirational reference later, not a starting point.

---

## 2. Launch strategy

1. **Fix the pilot blockers (2–3 weeks):** reseed hints and learning paths;
   configure a TURN relay for GDs; publish a DPDP-grade privacy notice that
   explains the camera and tab tracking; switch on nightly backups; rotate the
   Google OAuth secret; write a 1-page data-protection summary for IT teams.
2. **Validate content quality:** two or three faculty or alumni blind-review 30
   cases (realism, difficulty, rubric fairness). Publish the review result.
3. **Recruit 3–5 design-partner institutes** for free 8–10 week pilots.
4. **Convert to paid** for the next academic year with evidence from the pilot.

## 3. Pilot programme design

| Element | Design |
|---|---|
| Scope | One batch (60–120 students) + 1–2 faculty + the placement head |
| Duration | 8–10 weeks, ideally ending just before a placement season *(confirm each institute's calendar)* |
| Setup | Licence with email-domain auto-enrolment; 60-minute faculty training; placement-cell dashboard walkthrough |
| Cadence | Faculty set 1 assignment per week from the library or their own material; one proctored mock assessment mid-pilot; one GD session per week |
| Data collected | Activation, weekly active students, attempts per student, repeat-attempt score change, faculty marking time, AI-vs-faculty grade agreement, proctoring reopen rate, student survey (NPS + usefulness) |
| Commitments asked | A named champion; a 30-minute readout meeting; a reference or testimonial if targets are met |
| Exit | Readout with data → proposal for the next academic year |

**Pilot success targets (hypotheses to agree with each partner):**
≥ 70% of the batch complete ≥ 1 graded attempt; ≥ 40% weekly active in
weeks 2–8; median ≥ 6 attempts per active student; faculty report less
marking time than for a manual equivalent; AI–faculty grade correlation
reported, aiming for r ≥ 0.6 or better *(a target, not a claim)*.

## 4. Acquisition channels

| Channel | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct outreach to placement heads** | Primary | Warm intros via alumni, faculty contacts, LinkedIn; on-campus demo |
| **Faculty-first entry** | Primary | Free faculty accounts for case-based courses → classroom usage → institutional licence |
| **Placement-cell associations and B-school conferences** | Secondary | Regional placement-officer meets; a 20-minute live demo of the GD room and review queue |
| **Case competitions** | Awareness | Host a sponsored case competition on the platform (list it on Unstop, where students already look [S16]) |
| **Student ambassadors** | Usage, not sales | One per pilot campus: runs GD sessions, onboards peers, feeds back. Paid stipend or certificate |
| **Referrals** | After first wins | Discount on renewal for referring a sister institute; reference calls |
| **Content marketing** | Low-cost credibility | Free sample cases, "how placement GDs are scored" guides, aptitude drills; capture faculty and placement leads |
| **Partnerships (later)** | Distribution | Placement portals (Superset) or GD/PI training vendors that lack software; education groups with multiple campuses |
| **Digital ads** | Not recommended early | B2B buyers are few and reachable directly |

## 5. Demonstrations and trials

- **The 10-minute demo:** a student answers a case → per-criterion AI
  feedback in seconds → the same answer in the faculty review queue → the
  placement dashboard's "never started" list → a tab switch ends the test. It
  shows the college-facing loop, which is the differentiator.
- **Faculty sandbox:** a faculty account preloaded with a demo batch, so they
  can assign and mark without students.
- **Trial terms:** free pilot only with a named champion and agreed metrics, to avoid "free forever" pilots.

## 6. Converting pilots to paid

- Book the readout before the pilot starts.
- Present the institute's own numbers: activation, attempts, weak domains,
  faculty time.
- Offer the proposal priced for the next academic year with a founding-partner
  discount (e.g. 20–30% off year 1, in exchange for a case study).
- Ask for the PO before the budget closes *(timing to confirm per institute)*.

## 7. Phased roadmap

| Phase | Objectives | Key activities | KPIs | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Validation** (now → +6 weeks) | Confirm the problem and willingness to pay | 15–20 discovery calls with placement heads and faculty; competitor quotes; content blind review | Calls held; % reporting the problem as top-3; % willing to pilot; price reactions | ≥ 5 institutes willing to pilot; ≥ 3 give a price range ≥ ₹500/student |
| **2. MVP readiness** (parallel, 2–4 weeks) | Pilot-proof product | Hints/paths reseed, TURN relay, privacy notice, backups, demo sandbox, pilot analytics export | All pilot blockers closed; demo under 10 minutes | A faculty member can run an assignment end to end without help |
| **3. Pilot launch** (8–10 weeks) | Prove usage and value | 3–5 pilots per §3 | Activation, weekly active, attempts/student, AI–faculty agreement, marking time, NPS | ≥ 2 pilots meet the agreed targets |
| **4. First paying customers** (budget cycle) | Revenue and references | Readouts, proposals, founding-partner pricing | Pilots → paid conversion; ACV; days to PO | ≥ 2 paid contracts; ≥ 1 public case study |
| **5. Expansion across institutions** (year 1–2) | Repeatable sales in one cluster, then a second | Referrals, faculty champions, conference demos; first sales/support hire | Institutions, net revenue retention, CAC, sales cycle | ~15–20 institutions (base-case break-even) |
| **6. Long-term growth** | Grow beyond MBA | Size and test UG management, engineering placement readiness, recruiter assessments, international B-schools; LMS integration | ARR, gross margin, new-segment pilots | Second segment with paying customers |

**Do not claim** any partnership, pilot or customer until signed.
