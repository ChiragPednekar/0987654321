# Competitor analysis — CaseCode

*Phase 4. Web research done 13 October 2026. Source IDs [S#] refer to
`sources.md`. Pricing changes often and much of it sits behind "contact sales"
— every figure is marked with how it was obtained.*

**Rules used here**
- "Not found" means *not found in the sources reviewed*, never "they don't have it".
- Prices quoted from competitor blogs or aggregators are marked **(third-party)**.
- No competitor weakness is asserted without a source; where we only *infer*
  a gap, it is labelled as such.

---

## 1. Product category

**Primary category [Inference]:** B2B2C placement-preparation software for
business schools. Students use it, colleges license it. It sits where three
markets meet:

1. **Case-interview prep platforms** (global, mostly B2C, some B2B to universities)
2. **Indian placement-prep and GD/PI coaching** (B2C coaching institutes, B2B campus-training vendors)
3. **Assessment and proctoring software** (B2B, used by colleges and recruiters)

Plus two substitutes every buyer will raise: **free general-purpose AI**
(ChatGPT, Gemini) and **manual processes** (club casebooks, alumni mock
interviews, faculty marking by hand).

## 2. Competitor profiles

### 2.1 CaseCoach — closest B2B case-prep competitor (global)
- **Website:** casecoach.com · **Audience:** universities' career services and their students
- **Features (official page [S7]):** 100+ cases in varied formats; AI-powered
  practice drills (calculations, case math, structuring, chart
  interpretation), voice-based AI tools (per Yale listing [S7]); unlimited peer
  practice with students from the same and other client universities; live
  classes, workshops, train-the-trainer, one-to-one coaching; **an
  administration panel showing time spent on the platform and completed
  activities.**
- **Claimed reach:** "100+ leading universities in 20 countries", including HBS, Wharton, MIT Sloan, Cambridge [S7]
- **Business model:** institutional licence plus live events; pricing **not published** ("request a call back") [S7]. One signal: King's College London gives students free access with a university email and sells a CaseCoach-based course at £99 [S8].
- **Strengths:** brand built by former consultants; human-authored content; live events; elite school logos.
- **Gaps for our buyer [Inference, not verified]:** global consulting focus, not the Indian MBA placement format set (GD, WAT, PI); no faculty assignment-and-marking workflow found in the sources reviewed; likely priced for global schools.
- **Better than us:** credibility, content quality, existing university relationships, live coaching.
- **Where we differ:** Indian placement formats, faculty marking loop, proctored use as coursework, likely a far lower price point.

### 2.2 PrepLounge — peer-practice community (global, B2C)
- **Website:** preplounge.com · **Audience:** individual consulting candidates
- **Features:** peer-to-peer mock interviews (free tier: 10 peer meetings); Premium adds unlimited peer meetings, 200 cases, 200 drills, video solutions; separately sold coaching marketplace [S9 (third-party)].
- **Pricing (third-party):** Premium about $69 or €79 for one year (one-time) [S9]; coaching bundles from about €199 [S9]. The official pricing page could not be read in this review.
- **Strengths:** large community and network effects; marketplace of ex-consultant coaches.
- **Gaps [Inference]:** individual-focused; no institutional dashboard found in the sources reviewed.
- **Better than us:** community liquidity for peer practice; human coaches.

### 2.3 RocketBlocks — drills and coaching (global, B2C)
- **Website:** rocketblocks.me
- **Features:** math, chart and structuring drills; consulting and PM prep guides; ex-MBB coaches [S10].
- **Pricing:** coaching **$200/hour (official)** [S10]; annual drill pass about **$155/year (third-party)** [S10].
- **Better than us:** polished drill product; established reputation.
- **Where we differ:** full rubric-graded written cases, Indian formats, institutional tooling.

### 2.4 Road to Offer — AI-graded case practice (global, B2C)
- **Website:** roadtooffer.com
- **Features:** AI mock case interviews in voice or guided text, scored on a **seven-dimension rubric**; 600+ drill bank; BCG-style online-assessment simulator; resume grader [S11 (its own blog)].
- **Pricing (own blog):** $49/month, $120/90 days, $249/year, $20 starter bundle [S11].
- **Significance:** proves **rubric-based AI grading is not unique to us.** Our differentiation must rest on the institutional and Indian-format layers, not on "AI grades your case".
- **Better than us:** voice interview experience; consulting-specific depth; existing users (unverified count).

### 2.5 Management Consulted — courses and content (global, B2C)
- **Website:** managementconsulted.com
- **Pricing (official product pages):** Case Interview Course **$195**; Case Interview Bootcamp **$300** [S12].
- **Model:** one-off courses, case bank, coaching bundles.
- **Relevance:** sets the international price anchor for self-study prep (₹17,000–₹26,000 equivalent).

### 2.6 Big Interview — AI interview practice, sold to universities (global)
- **Website:** biginterview.com
- **Features:** AI-powered interview practice and resume tools; institutional licensing [S13].
- **Pricing (official teams page [S13]):** up to 50 learners **$975/year**; 100 learners **$1,500/year**; 200 learners **$2,200/year**; monthly $9.75/user. Enterprise quote-based; claims 700+ organisations including universities.
- **Significance:** the clearest **published per-learner institutional price** in this review: about $11–20 per learner per year (≈ ₹970–₹1,700 at ₹88/USD).
- **Gap for our buyer [Inference]:** general behavioural interviews; no case, GD or Indian placement formats found in the sources reviewed.

### 2.7 Yoodli — AI role-play and communication coaching (global)
- **Website:** yoodli.ai · **Audience:** enterprises; also used by universities, by its own account [S14]
- **Features:** AI role-play for conversations; delivery feedback [S14]. Raised at a reported $300M valuation (TechCrunch, Dec 2025 [S14]).
- **Pricing:** enterprise plans quote-based; a third-party listing shows from about $10 [S14].
- **Significance:** a well-funded AI role-play company could move into sales/negotiation role-play for business schools.

### 2.8 VMock — AI career platform licensed to universities (global, India office)
- **Website:** vmock.com
- **Features:** resume scoring against 100+ parameters, interview practice, LinkedIn optimisation [S15].
- **Model:** **institutional licence fee** (co-founder interview, 2021) [S15]; used by Indian B-schools, e.g. Jaipuria [S25].
- **Pricing:** not published.
- **Significance:** proof that Indian B-schools **already pay for AI career tools** through the institution. A precedent for our sale and a likely incumbent at target colleges.

### 2.9 Unstop — India's student-engagement and hiring platform
- **Website:** unstop.com
- **Features:** competitions, hackathons, quizzes, internships, jobs; assessments and proctoring for recruiters; colleges and societies host events free [S16].
- **Pricing:** free for students; **Unstop Pro ₹1,999/year** individual subscription (launch article, undated) [S16]; recruiter pricing quote-based.
- **Significance:** it owns the Indian student's attention for case competitions. It is a channel (to list competitions) as much as a competitor.
- **Better than us:** reach (claims 17M+ students [S16]), brand, recruiter network.

### 2.10 Superset — placement management (India)
- **Website:** joinsuperset.com
- **What it is:** the placement *portal* for 600+ Indian colleges (official claim) — student profiles, applications, recruiter outreach, scheduling, analytics [S17]. Named users include IIM-A, IIM-C, IIM-B, BITS Pilani [S17].
- **Relationship to us:** **complementary, not competing.** Superset runs placements; it does not train students. An integration or export would be a selling point. Also a potential partner or acquirer.

### 2.11 Mercer | Mettl and Talview — assessment and proctoring (India/global)
- **Websites:** mettl.com, talview.com
- **Features:** online exams, AI and human proctoring, campus hiring assessments, LMS integrations (Mettl lists Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, D2L) [S18][S19].
- **Pricing:** quote / usage-based [S18].
- **India evidence:** Mettl case study of a "leading Indian management institute" moving exams off Zoom to AI + human proctoring [S18].
- **Significance:** colleges already pay for proctoring. Our proctoring is bundled with practice rather than sold per exam. **But theirs is more mature** (human proctors, ID verification, certifications) [S19].

### 2.12 Coaching institutes' GD/PI/WAT programmes (India)
- **IMS:** GDPI 2026 programme **₹6,000 + GST**; PI-only **₹2,500 + GST** (official support page [S20]).
- **Career Launcher:** GD-PI-WAT programme; listed price unclear on the page reviewed [S21].
- **Relevance:** students already pay ₹2,500–₹6,000+ for GD/PI prep **before** admission. This is a B2C anchor for the value of these formats. These programmes target admission interviews, not placement interviews.

### 2.13 Campus training vendors (India)
- **FACE Prep:** placement training for colleges; claims 2,000+ partner institutions [S22]. A 2018 university contract priced aptitude/soft-skills training at **₹33 per student per hour + GST** [S22]. Old, but shows that colleges buy placement training **per student**.
- **InsideIIM:** free placement-prep content; paid internship courses at **₹2,499** each (older page) [S23].
- **AceMBA:** case-competition and placement training for MBAs [S26]; pricing not reviewed.

### 2.14 In-house AI tools at B-schools
- **Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham** launched an AI mock-interview platform for management aspirants (March 2026) [S24].
- **Jaipuria** runs an "AI-powered placement lab" combining VMock, Rezi and other tools [S25].
- **Significance:** demand exists. Some colleges will build their own or assemble point tools — the "do it yourself" competitor.

### 2.15 General-purpose AI (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) — the biggest substitute
A student can paste a case into a free AI tool and ask for rubric feedback.
Product-linked guides concede free AI is "genuinely strong" at drills and
structuring, and weaker at grading without bias and at pressure [S29].
**What we add [Fact]:** a fixed rubric per case, consistent scoring, history
and progress, proctored conditions, and the faculty and placement-cell
layer. **Honest answer:** for an individual student practising alone, the gap
is narrowing. For a **college** that needs consistency, oversight and
integrity, a free chatbot is not a substitute. That is why the institutional
model is the right one.

### 2.16 Manual processes — the real incumbent at most colleges
Consulting-club casebooks, alumni and senior mock interviews, faculty-run
GD/PI sessions, spreadsheets of who attended. **[Inference — no source]:**
free, trusted and personal, but they do not scale, are not measured, and
depend on volunteers. Pilot data should quantify this, for example faculty
hours per mock-interview round.

## 4. Competitive positioning

### Why a customer would choose us instead of…

| Competitor | Reason to choose CaseCode | Honest counter-argument |
|---|---|---|
| CaseCoach | Indian placement formats (GD, WAT, PI, aptitude) and faculty marking; likely much cheaper | CaseCoach's content and brand are proven; ours are not |
| PrepLounge / RocketBlocks / Road to Offer | The college pays; faculty and placement cell get visibility; proctored coursework | Better B2C products for a motivated individual |
| Big Interview / Yoodli / VMock | Case, GD and Indian MBA formats, not generic interviews | Established vendors, possibly already licensed by the college |
| Unstop | Structured, graded practice rather than a listing/competition marketplace | Unstop has the students' attention already |
| Mettl / Talview | Practice and proctoring in one tool, no per-exam fee | Their proctoring is more mature (human review, ID checks) |
| Coaching institutes | Always-on, every student, institution-wide | Human coaches; strong brands |
| ChatGPT | Consistency, rubric, records, faculty oversight, integrity | Free and improving fast |
| Manual process | Scales to the whole batch; measured | Personal, trusted, free |

### Strongest defensible differentiator
**The college-facing loop around practice:** practice → assignment →
AI pre-grade → faculty mark → placement-cell visibility, run under
exam-grade integrity, across the Indian placement format set. Each piece
exists somewhere. **The combination, priced for Indian B-schools, was not
found in any reviewed competitor.** *(This is a finding of this review, not
proof that no such product exists.)*

### Table stakes (do not lead with these)
AI case grading, AI mock interviews, drills, leaderboards, streaks and badges,
resume review, company prep pages.

### Genuinely distinctive (today, in code)
1. Faculty assignment-and-marking workflow with the AI grade shown as advisory.
2. Live multi-student GD room with AI scoring per participant.
3. On-device camera proctoring with server-enforced lockouts, inside a *practice* platform.
4. Negotiation, sales and business-simulation role-plays for MBA placements.
5. Data hosted in India (Mumbai) — relevant to DPDP-conscious institutions.

### What are we competing on?
**Specialisation and outcomes for a specific buyer** (Indian B-schools), with
**price** as the enabler. Not on content prestige, not on UX alone.

### What stops an incumbent copying us?
Honestly: **little, technically.** Any of these features can be rebuilt in
months. What can become defensible over time:
- **Institutional embedding:** faculty-authored cases, assignment history and
  gradebooks make a college's switching cost real.
- **Calibration data:** AI-vs-faculty grade pairs from pilots — a dataset no
  B2C competitor collects — to make grading measurably trustworthy.
- **Indian placement content** reviewed by faculty and alumni at partner colleges.
- **Price structure** that global players are unlikely to match in India.

### Positioning statement (proposed)
> For Indian business schools that need every student placement-ready,
> CaseCode is the placement-practice platform that faculty can assign, mark
> and trust — covering cases, GDs, interviews and aptitude under exam-grade
> integrity, in one licence. Unlike global case-prep tools built for
> individual consulting aspirants, CaseCode is built for the whole batch and
> the people accountable for its placements.

## 5. SWOT

| **Strengths** | **Weaknesses** |
|---|---|
| Broad, working feature set for Indian placement formats | **Zero users, pilots, revenue or outcome data** |
| Faculty and placement-cell tooling built in | AI-generated content, not expert-reviewed |
| On-device proctoring, no per-exam cost | Hints and learning-path content missing after the rebuild |
| Measured unit costs; quota and cost caps built in | GD needs a TURN relay for college networks |
| Data hosted in India; strong security hardening | No LMS/SSO integration; no certifications |
| Very low infrastructure cost | Team, entity and support capacity undocumented |
| **Opportunities** | **Threats** |
| ~4.2 lakh approved management seats a year [S1]; many colleges lack structured prep | Free general AI good enough for motivated students |
| Colleges already pay for AI career tools (VMock) and proctoring (Mettl) [S15][S18] | Global players (CaseCoach, Big Interview, Yoodli) entering India |
| DPDP Rules push colleges toward compliant, India-hosted vendors [S27] | Long, relationship-driven college procurement |
| Faculty-authored content becomes a moat | AI costs double on 1 Jan 2027 [S6] |
| Partnerships with placement portals (Superset) or coaching brands | Privacy pushback on camera proctoring |
