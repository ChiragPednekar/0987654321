Source-of-truth research for the CaseCode pitch deck, prepared 13 October 2026.

| `stakeholder_value_and_risks.md` | Stakeholder value matrix, pilot KPIs, risk register, ten questions | 9–10 |
| `pitch_deck_blueprint.md` | 12-slide outline with message, evidence, visuals and claims to validate | 11 |

prices and projections are hypotheses; "not found" means not found in the
sources reviewed, never "does not exist".
